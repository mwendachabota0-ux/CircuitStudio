---
name: Circuit Studio working notes
description: Ownership and update behavior for the circuit builder's living brief.
---

Working notes are the circuit's living brief: its goal, requirements, assumptions, and open questions. The AI should use them as context and update them as the conversation develops, while preserving user edits and leaving every section editable.

**Why:** The user wants the AI to maintain useful circuit context without taking away the user's control of the notes.

**How to apply:** Keep AI-generated brief content distinct from the current merged notes so new AI details can be added without silently replacing saved user changes.
