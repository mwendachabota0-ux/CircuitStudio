---
name: Circuit Studio interface restraint
description: Keep copy short and interaction panels visually unboxed.
---

Keep user-facing copy short and action-focused; do not narrate internal AI workflow. Use cards for selectable, editable, or otherwise grouped items when they make scanning and interaction easier. Avoid wrapping every section or control in bordered or tinted containers. Put optional privacy and process details behind help; keep essential safety warnings, current status, destructive-action scope, and applied/not-applied state visible.

**Why:** The user wants less interface explanation and fewer decorative containers, while retaining useful cards that make interaction clearer.

**How to apply:** Add a card only when it communicates a discrete item or action group. Keep card styling restrained, remove copy that does not aid a task or decision, and preserve concise safety, privacy, status, and destructive-action context. Keep Studio and KiCad library cards consistent in size, layout, preview fit, descriptions, and actions; add KiCad-specific source details without removing shared card features.
